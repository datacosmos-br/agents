# ADR-0024 — FLEXT generator declaration authority

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-20; authority routing amended 2026-10-20.

## Context

The original intake copied FLEXT generator law into this catalog and used a
foreign three-digit ADR identifier that the bundle could not resolve. This
record preserves the approval-format lesson. Git history retains the imported
articles and their former global rule.

## Decision

Global approval tags continue to resolve physically to this repository's
four-digit ADR records. That format does not transfer authorship of a project's
law into the global catalog.

The copied global generator-declarations rule is retired. FLEXT's active
branch-matched ADR-018 and local law own its generator contract;
flext-development resolves that authority. Universal source/projection conduct
remains at workflow/generators-not-projections and canonical-commands.

## Consequences

A project decision is consumed from its matching branch rather than copied into
another normative body. Source validation, integration and installed withdrawal
remain separate receipts; retirement in this catalog does not prove publication.
