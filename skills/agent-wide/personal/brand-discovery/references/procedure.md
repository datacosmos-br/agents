# Brand identity interview and synthesis

## Preflight

Resolve the brand, decision this work must support, participants and their authority,
existing source material, session format, required deliverable, and whether persistence
was requested. When a durable destination was requested, validate its physical owner,
existing content, privacy boundary, and write authorization before the first effect.

Missing participants or decision purpose blocks the interview. Missing evidence does not
authorize a generic identity, archetype, audience, or positioning statement.

## Interview modules

Work in the smallest useful module and summarize current state before resuming a later
session:

1. **Purpose and boundaries:** founding conviction, behavior it requires, and what the
   brand refuses to do.
2. **Positioning:** target situation, alternatives, differentiated value, credible
   proof, and the position the organization intends to own.
3. **Audience:** decision maker, trigger, prior attempts, desired outcome,
   disqualifiers, and evidence from real customers.
4. **Personality:** desired character, behavioral examples, tensions, and explicit
   anti-character.
5. **Voice context:** admired and rejected real samples, stable voice traits, and
   contextual tone changes. Hand corpus analysis to `brand-voice`.
6. **Narrative:** origin, conflict, transformation, customer as beneficiary, and
   invitation.
7. **Founder and organization:** current dependency on founder reputation, desired
   boundary, transition evidence, and risk.

Ask from concrete stories toward abstractions. Capture attributed raw statements before
interpretations. Keep unresolved disagreement visible by participant and ask the
decision owner to resolve only choices that materially change the brief.

## Synthesis contract

Produce one concise briefing packet:

- evidence summary and unresolved contradictions;
- purpose and behavioral principles;
- target audience and disqualifiers;
- category, alternatives, differentiated value, and proof;
- personality and anti-personality;
- narrative and founder/organization boundary;
- positioning brief for competitive research;
- inputs handed to `brand-voice` for a source-derived voice profile;
- open decisions and confidence limits.

Do not present interview preference as market fact. A positioning hypothesis becomes
externally supported only after the selected research owner validates it.

## Persistence and completion

When persistence is authorized, re-read the destination immediately before the write,
preserve compatible current content, and publish the complete updated artifact
atomically. Record module status in that same owner; do not create a parallel checkpoint
database or fixed home-directory convention.

Completion requires every requested module, attributed evidence, explicit
contradictions, a decision-owner disposition for blocking conflicts, and a reviewed
synthesis. Otherwise return the first missing input and the completed modules without
claiming a finished brandbook.
