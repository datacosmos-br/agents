# Brand Voice

Build a durable voice profile from real source material, then reuse that profile
everywhere instead of re-deriving style from scratch or defaulting to generic AI copy.
Follow the complete `router procedure` (skill file) and preserve its owners, evidence
contracts, failure propagation, and required output standard.
