# Product capability inputs

Read the smallest relevant subset of these sources:

1. Product intent: issue, discussion, PRD, roadmap note, or operator decision.
2. Current architecture: repository documentation, contracts, schemas, routes,
   and existing workflows.
3. Capability context: product manifests, design documents, RFCs, migration
   notes, and operating-model records.
4. Delivery constraints: authorization, billing, compliance, rollout,
   compatibility, performance, and review policy.

Classify every statement as approved product truth, observed implementation,
engineering preference, or unresolved question. Do not promote an inference to
approved policy.
