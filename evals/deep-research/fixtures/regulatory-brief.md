# Decision brief

Decision: whether a European SaaS vendor that integrates a third-party general-purpose AI model needs a 2026 compliance workstream under the EU AI Act.

Scope:
- distinguish duties of the upstream GPAI provider from those of the SaaS deployer;
- identify obligations already applicable and dated future milestones;
- prioritize EU institutions, enacted text, and regulator guidance;
- flag disputed interpretations instead of resolving them by assertion.

Audience: product counsel and engineering leadership.
