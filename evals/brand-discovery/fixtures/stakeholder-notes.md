# Northstar stakeholder notes

Decision: prepare a positioning brief before choosing a new website direction. Decision
owner: Ana, CEO. Participants: Ana (CEO) and Rui (sales lead).

- Ana: “We exist to make warehouse maintenance understandable before a failure becomes
  an emergency.”
- Rui: “Buyers call us when spreadsheets stop showing who owns the next action.”
- Ana wants to position Northstar as the operational clarity platform for maintenance
  leaders.
- Rui believes buyers only understand the narrower category “maintenance task tracker”
  and wants that phrase in the headline.
- Best-fit customer: multi-site warehouse operator with a maintenance manager and
  recurring handoff failures.
- Worst fit: a single-site team seeking only a generic checklist.
- Desired character: calm, exact, prepared. Never alarmist or playful about safety
  incidents.
- Founding story: Ana missed a critical handoff while managing three sites and built the
  first workflow to make responsibility visible.
- Founder boundary: current sales depend on Ana's demos, but the product must be
  credible without her within twelve months.
- Existing website copy samples are available separately for corpus analysis.
