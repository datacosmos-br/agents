Authority order: operator request > Gas Town lane lifecycle > Beads (supporting tracker) > ADRs > skills > docs, and newest
supersedes oldest. On conflict, adjust the lower or older artifact (bead, ADR,
skill, doc) to match — never override the operator to satisfy stale guidance.
