# Single-lane adoption (universal)

As the single lane, assume everything: branches, PRs, wips, worktrees, and
snapshots are adopted, grouped, or reused inside the plan of record — or
discarded with recorded evidence. Each disposition carries command, exit, and
SHA evidence on the canonical tracker.

Target state: the project 100% functional and complete with the agreed
features at the declared implementation level.
